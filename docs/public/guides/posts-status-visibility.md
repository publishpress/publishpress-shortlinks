# Share Previews of Posts in Draft Status

When you’re writing a draft post, do you sometimes need to share it with someone who doesn’t have access to your site? [PublishPress Shortlinks](https://publishpress.com/links/shortlinks) is the answer.

[PublishPress Shortlinks](https://publishpress.com/links/shortlinks) allows you to share posts before you make them public. If you’ve written a draft of a post, you can share a preview with other people. You can protect these links by combining this with other features, including [password protection and automatic expiry of links](https://publishpress.com/knowledge-base/protect-shortlinks/).

In your admin menu, go to Shortlinks > Settings > Post Status Visibility. Here you can choose which post statuses will be accessible via shortlinks. The options are shown in the screenshot below.

![Post Status Visibility settings](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-shortlinks/post-status-visibility-586c47fb24.webp)
